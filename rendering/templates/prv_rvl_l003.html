Dear {{ fields.provider_name }}:

In accordance with the Affordable Care Act, the New Hampshire Medicaid Program requires providers to verify enrollment information at least every five years. This is called revalidation. You were notified by two previous letters that you had been selected to revalidate your enrollment information.

Failure to complete your revalidation by {{ fields.revalidation_due_date }} will result in the termination of your enrollment in the NH Medicaid program.

To initiate the revalidation process, please visit www.nhmmis.nh.gov. Once logged in, select Revalidation from the Quick Links and an instruction page will be presented. Please click on Start Revalidation located in the bottom right-hand corner and your current enrollment data will be displayed. You will be given the option to either confirm or update your information. If you have started the revalidation process and have not completed it, please do so immediately.
